## Notes and limitations

- A property can be advertised for both sale and rent. Listing-type counts
  describe each row's site-provided classification.
- There are no repeated property IDs in this snapshot.
- Coordinates come from listing data and may be approximate. Listings without
  both latitude and longitude are not present in this version of the dataset.
- Province values are derived from listing addresses and may be incomplete.
- These are point-in-time counts; they may change when the dataset is updated.
- Confirm permission and applicable terms before redistributing scraped
  listing data publicly.
